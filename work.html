Driving growth at CIRQL, from research to roadmap

Product & strategy Product Management & Strategy Associate, CIRQL Apr – Sep 2026

The brief

CIRQL is an early-stage event-discovery and campus-social app, built in Flutter, Dart and Firebase, heading towards pre-revenue investment rounds. The team needed to know where it stood against established platforms, what users were struggling with, and where to spend the months ahead.

What I did

  • Ran an AI-assisted competitor and market-positioning analysis across 10+ comparable platforms, including Eventbrite, Meetup and Ticketmaster UK, completing the framework and analysis stage in one to two hours against a full day of manual research.
  • Surfaced differentiation gaps that recalibrated the 2026 roadmap: the user-acquisition push moved from April to August so the team could prioritise application development and UI/UX work first.
  • Synthesised user feedback on interface issues, from inconsistent iconography and low-resolution branding to unintuitive go-live and event-selection flows, into a prioritised design and development programme spanning the interface, landing page and QR-code journey.
  • Directed AI-assisted drafting of landing-page and link-in-bio copy through five revision passes, correcting AI-introduced errors along the way, and implemented dynamic QR codes across distribution channels for location-level engagement analytics.
  • Built working fluency in the app's codebase through AI-assisted code interpretation so I could contribute substantively to feature scoping, technical feasibility and prioritisation.
  • Produced and hosted around 35 live-streamed events, networked directly at live events to onboard users, and sourced event-organiser partnerships to strengthen the supply side of the platform.

What came of it

20% community growth in the early launch phase 50+ users onboarded by direct networking 10+ event-organiser partnerships ~50% less copy-production time, at lower cost than a commercial tool

Taking the V&A Living Wage campaign to Tower Hamlets Town Hall

Policy & organising Civil Society Organising Intern, Citizens UK (TELCO) Jan – Apr 2026

The brief

Citizens UK organises across East London through an alliance of faith institutions, schools, universities, trade unions, councils and grassroots networks. The portfolio I supported spanned the Living Wage, Living Hours, temporary accommodation, ESOL provision, air quality and youth safety. The Living Wage campaign at the V&A needed communications that would carry student voices into a formal accountability submission to the Mayor of London.

What I did

  • Designed and delivered the campaign communications end to end: scripted, filmed and edited student testimony and supported the formal accountability submission to the Mayor of London at Tower Hamlets Town Hall.
  • Managed turnout strategy, delegate tracking and operational delivery for the TELCO Delegates Assembly, the National Delegates Assembly and the Issues Workshop, and chaired the Safety panel.
  • Used AI tools to draft and restructure running orders and speech timings for 10+ campaign events, from nationwide asks to borough-level actions, and to convert 10+ pieces of policy and campaign material into public-facing posters and adverts, keeping manual review for audience fit.
  • Deployed CRM-style tracking to monitor engagement and reinforce institutional accountability across member organisations.

What came of it

Mayor of London received the formal accountability submission 10+ campaign events with running orders drafted ~25% faster materials production, with review kept in the loop

Coal market intelligence for an Indian infrastructure firm

Strategy Strategy Associate, Varaha Infra Limited Sep 2025 – Jun 2026

The brief

Senior leadership needed a steady, structured view of the Indian coal and energy sector, from pricing and logistics to regulation, to inform forward planning, risk and capital allocation, plus a UK-based point of contact for investors.

What I did

  • Managed and interpreted commercial datasets in Excel spanning coal pricing, logistics costs and supply-chain volumes, turning raw figures into reporting that informed executive oversight and capital-allocation decisions.
  • Built and ran a recurring AI-automated research workflow, using ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, that tracked regulatory, pricing and supply-chain developments from September 2025 to June 2026 and consolidated them into structured reports.
  • Assessed expansion viability into Madhya Pradesh and Gujarat and their principal industrial centres, weighing cost structures, supply-chain economics and state renewable-energy incentives such as electricity-duty exemptions, stamp-duty reimbursements and wheeling-charge waivers.
  • Served as the UK liaison for investors and external stakeholders, coordinating bilateral meetings with leadership travelling from India, and prepared briefing materials and investor-facing decks.

What came of it

10 months of continuous sector reporting to leadership Two states assessed for expansion, with findings retained for future consideration

An AI project-management automation and a go/no-go business case

Project management Project Manager (consulting practicum), Mayerfeld Consulting Aug – Sep 2026

The brief

Two deliverables in four weeks: an AI-based project-management tool that met the practicum's requirement, and a founder-ready feasibility case for a proposed 2027 pilot of Formula Africa World, a pan-African youth leadership initiative combining two learning formats.

What I did

  • Architected and deployed an automation in Make.com connecting Gemini, Trello and Gmail via API: AI-generated meeting notes, such as those from Zoom and Google Meet, become task cards with due dates and labels, paired with automated status emails to the people responsible. Each API connection was configured and tested, and the workflow deployed via Network Solutions hosting.
  • Led a five-person team through the full lifecycle from kick-off to final recommendation, authoring the project charter, goal statement, assumptions log, RACI matrix, team charter and milestone roadmap.
  • Built the stakeholder register, engagement strategy and communication plan for investors, trainers and target communities, which shaped how the pilot was positioned to each audience.
  • Developed the work breakdown structure with three-point and PERT estimation, sequenced work packages and dependencies into a realistic launch timeline, and ran a risk and opportunity register with qualitative and quantitative analysis.
  • Synthesised the analysis into a feasibility business case covering pilot benefits, commercial cooperation, cohort volume, resourcing and funding, presented directly to programme stakeholders.

What came of it

Deployed a working automation, meeting the practicum requirement Go or no-go business case presented for the 2027 pilot Five-person team led over four weeks
