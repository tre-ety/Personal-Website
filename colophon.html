How this site is made.

A colophon: what the site is built with, the decisions behind how it looks, and what it does and doesn't do with your data.

Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript. There is no framework, no build step and no dependency to install: every page is a file you could open from a folder. The whole site is hand-written, with AI assistance used for drafting and review in the same way it is used in my work, with a person deciding what ships.

It is hosted on GitHub Pages from a public repository, tre-ety/Personal-Website, so every change has a commit behind it.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque

One typeface, Bricolage Grotesque by Mathieu Triay, used across its variable axes: condensed and heavy for display, regular widths for reading. One family chosen deliberately does more than two chosen by habit. It is the only third-party resource the site loads.

Colour

Ground#FBFBFA
Ink#0D0B0A
Amber#EDB183
Teal#1E555C
Plum#D4636E

A near-white ground with near-black ink, and three accents that each mean something: amber for product and strategy, plum for policy and organising, teal for communications. The accents are used flat, as whole section plates and as dots and chips, never as gradients. Where they appear as text they are darkened so every line meets WCAG AA contrast.

The mark

A lowercase j whose dot is a lens ring: the j of Jio, the o of Ong, and the camera, in one shape that still reads at sixteen pixels. It is the favicon, the home-screen icon and the badge on the link-preview image.

Motion

Motion is used where it says something. On the homepage the name types itself out, a field of specks gathers into a slowly turning ring around your pointer, a photo reel crossfades beneath the heading, the places I have worked drift past in a strip, a short statement types itself as you reach it, and each piece of selected work holds its place while its card scrolls by. The reel and the strip both have pause buttons. If your system asks for reduced motion, all of it stops and the page is simply still.

Accessibility

  • Text contrast meets WCAG AA throughout, measured, not assumed.
  • Everything works from the keyboard, with a visible focus ring and a skip link on every page.
  • The photo viewer keeps focus inside itself while open and returns it when closed.
  • Every photograph has a written description.

Your data

The site has no analytics, no cookies and no tracking of any kind. The only request that leaves it is for the typeface from Google Fonts. The contact buttons open your own email client; nothing is collected here.
