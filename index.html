About

I work at the meeting point of product, strategy and politics. As Product Owner at Car Planet UK, I own the backlog, roadmap and release testing for a portfolio of in-house web, mobile and AI products used daily across the business, prioritising requests from sales, operations, finance and HR into one roadmap for a single development team. Before that I was a Product Management and Strategy Associate at CIRQL, turning competitor analysis and user feedback into roadmap decisions and driving growth at the coalface.

That product instinct grew out of a political education. I am a Politics graduate of Queen Mary University of London, with Upper Second Class Honours and a dissertation on how disinformation, voter predispositions and contested historical memory shaped the 2022 Philippine presidential election.

Alongside this I serve as Regional Communications, Media and PR Officer for St John Ambulance, have organised in East London with Citizens UK, and spent four years as a freelance open-source investigator. I research, build, write, film and organise.
